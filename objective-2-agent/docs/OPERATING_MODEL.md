# Operating model

- Deanne approves scope and sprint exit gates.
- GPT and Claude act as coding and review collaborators.
- Work is organized into bounded sprints with explicit deliverables, tests, dependencies, and exit gates.
- Decisions, handoffs, and reviews are logged in the Notion Collaborative Developer Log.
- Formal executions are recorded in the Notion Test Run Audit Log with commit, environment, command, inputs, outputs, and metrics.
- No sprint is complete until its deliverables are committed and its exit gate is satisfied.
